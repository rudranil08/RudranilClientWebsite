Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website or contact me. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Who I am

I'm Rudranil Dhol, a freelance web developer based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. I'm the person responsible for this website and for any personal details you share with me. You can reach me at rudranildhol@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 63826 31521.

What this website collects

This website does not collect personal information by itself. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising tools, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts. The fonts are hosted on this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other font service.

My hosting provider, Vercel, delivers this site to you. Like any web host, it may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure. I don't use that information to identify you.

The contact form

When you fill in the form, what you type stays in your browser. The form only prepares a WhatsApp message. It is not sent to me, or stored anywhere, until you choose to send it in WhatsApp. If you do, WhatsApp (operated by Meta) handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

The form asks only for what I need to reply: your name, your business name (optional), the type of business, what you need, and an optional note. Please don't include sensitive details such as bank or ID numbers.

What I do with messages you send me

  • I use them to reply to you, prepare a quote, and do the work if we agree to work together.
  • I don't sell your details or share them with anyone else, except people who help me deliver your project (for example, a domain registrar) and only when you've agreed.
  • I keep project messages and payment records while we work together and for a reasonable time afterwards (for example, to resolve questions about payments), then delete them.
  • If we don't work together, I delete your enquiry within a few months.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use similar tracking technologies such as local storage for tracking. Because there are no cookies to ask you about, there is no cookie banner. If I ever add analytics or anything that uses cookies, I'll update this page first and ask for your consent where needed.

If I contact you first

I may message local businesses on WhatsApp or by email to introduce my services, using contact details the business has published publicly. Every message tells you how to opt out. Reply "stop" or email me and I'll stop contacting you and won't message you again.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information of yours I hold,
  • correct anything that's wrong, or
  • delete your information (a data deletion request).

Email rudranildhol@gmail.com with "Delete my data" in the subject. I'll confirm by reply and complete the request within 30 days, unless I need to keep something for a legal or accounting reason, in which case I'll tell you what and why.

Children

This website is for business owners and adults. It is not aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent me their details, tell me and I'll delete them.

Changes to this page

If I change how I handle information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
