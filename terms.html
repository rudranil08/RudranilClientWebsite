Terms and refund policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

These are the simple ground rules for website projects I do. The details of each project (price, pages, features, dates) are always confirmed in a written quote before you pay anything.

The quote

  • Prices on this site are starting prices ("from"). The final price depends on what you need and is confirmed in writing before I start.
  • Your quote lists exactly what's included. Anything not listed is extra, and I'll tell you the price before doing it.
  • Costs paid to other companies, such as a domain name, paid hosting or paid plugins, are not included unless the quote says so. They are bought in your name.

Payment

  • 50% is paid in advance to start work. The remaining 50% is paid before I make the finished site live or hand over the files.
  • I accept UPI and bank transfer.
  • Monthly care plans, if you choose one, are billed monthly. You can stop at any time by giving me 7 days' notice.

What I need from you

  • Your text, photos, logo and other content, in good time. Delays in sending them can move the delivery date.
  • Confirmation that you own, or have permission to use, everything you give me. I can't use images or text that belong to someone else without their permission.

Revisions

Two rounds of changes are included in every project. Extra rounds, or changes to what was agreed in the quote, are priced separately and agreed before I start.

Ownership

  • When you've paid in full, you own the finished website, and I hand over the files and access.
  • I may show the finished project in my portfolio. If you'd rather I didn't, tell me before we start and I won't.

Fixes after launch

If something I built doesn't work as agreed, I'll fix it free of charge when you report it within 14 days of launch. New features and changes after that are quoted separately, or covered by a monthly care plan.

What I can't promise

I'll set up your site so Google can read and list it, but I can't guarantee a particular search ranking, number of visitors or sales. Results depend on many things I don't control.

Refund policy

  • Before I start work: if you change your mind, I'll refund your advance in full when you tell me within 3 days of paying, as long as I haven't begun.
  • After I've started: the advance covers time I've set aside and work already done, so it isn't refundable. If you cancel part-way, you pay for the work completed up to that point. If you've paid more than that, I'll refund the difference.
  • If I can't deliver: if I'm unable to deliver the project as agreed, for a reason that's on my side, I'll refund everything you've paid.
  • After delivery: once the finished work is delivered and final payment is made, it isn't refundable. If something doesn't work as agreed, I'll fix it (see "Fixes after launch" above).
  • Refunds are paid back to the account you paid from, usually within 7 days.

Liability

I'll do careful, professional work, but I can't be responsible for losses that come from things outside my control, such as problems with your hosting company, a domain expiring, or content you supplied. My total responsibility for any project is limited to the amount you paid me for it.

Disagreements

If something goes wrong, please talk to me first. I'd like to fix it. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
