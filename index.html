  2. You approve a plan

    I send a one-page quote with what's included, the price and the date. You pay 50% to start.

Questions people ask

How long does it take?

A Starter site usually takes about 5 days and a Business site about 7 to 10 days, once I have your photos and details.

I don't have photos or a logo. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I can start with clean placeholder images and you can swap in real photos later, and I can make a simple logo for a small extra fee if you need one.

Will I be able to change things later?

Yes. Small changes like prices, timings and photos are covered by the monthly care plan, or I can do them as one-off jobs. You'll also get a short guide on how everything works.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is bought in your name, and I hand over everything when the final payment is made.

How do I pay?

UPI or bank transfer. I take 50% before I start and 50% when you approve the finished work.

Will people actually find my website on Google?

I set up the basics so Google can read your site and show it with your name, area and Google Maps location. Ranking at the top depends on competition, and I'll be honest with you about what to expect.
